Methodology

The method is now methodology-v2.md (2026-09-30), adopted after two outside reviews. The reasons for every change are in amendments.md; the coding rules with examples are in codebook.md.

The version 1 method (one stance code, six question frames, the "prominent enough" panel rule) was written from the kickoff plan the same day and is retired. It remains readable in git history (this file at commit 1508fde) and in the method sections of 2026-09-30-kickoff.md.

Points of detail not spelled out in version 2

These come from version 1 and still apply; the agent implementation entry in amendments.md lists the rest.

  • Dataset window: statements from 2005-01-01 onward.
  • Fixed query set for each panel member (logged in data/search_log.csv): discovery queries D1 "<name>" AGI prediction, D2 "<name>" "artificial general intelligence", D3 "<name>" "human-level" AI when; then for every year from the first documented year to the present, Y1 "<name>" AGI <year>, Y2 "<name>" "human-level" intelligence <year>, Y3 "<name>" superintelligence OR singularity OR "self-improving" <year>; plus the person's own outlets. Quiet years are logged as rows with what_found "quiet year: no qualifying statement found".
  • Busy years: more than ten qualifying statements → keep every timeline-bearing one, sample the rest at every third by date, tag sampled, log the sampling.
  • events.csv fields: event_id, date, date_precision, event_type (open_letter, public_statement, government_action, report), title, description, source_url, notes. Epoch AI reports are report events. Searches behind it use speaker_id events in the search log.
  • aggregates.csv holds the survey, market and poll rows collected in the seed. Under version 2 section 4, population survey items move to data/surveys.csv when the population stream starts; that move has not been made yet.
  • National outlet (for the panel prominence test): a national newspaper, broadcaster, news agency or national magazine, including science and technology magazines such as Wired and New Scientist; not blogs, newsletters, trade press or the person's own outlets.
  • Frontier lab tie: frontier_lab_tie is yes when the person led or ran a company building the most capable models of the time during any statement in the record; the years are given in role_summary.
