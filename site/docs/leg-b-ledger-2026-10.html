Leg B ledger: first reading, 9 October 2026

Method: docs/leg-b-ledger-method.md (committed before any row was built). Rows: data/leg_b_ledger.csv. Numbers: python3 scripts/leg_b_ledger.py. Every verdict is the agent's judgement; the rows record the evidence and the gap so John can overrule any one. Revised the same day after an independent check of the pull request, and closed by John's rulings of 12 October 2026 (both at the end).

The answer

The singularity definition said the 2016 survey's milestones "mostly arrived early". The ledger does not support that as written. Every milestone that has arrived came early, but more milestones are past their forecast date and still not here than have arrived at all. (The sentence was amended on 12 October 2026, amendment A23 in docs/amendments.md; see the rulings at the end.)

verdict count
arrived (every stated condition met) 1
arguably arrived (main capability met, exactly one condition unmet or untested) 5
not arrived 22
no bar (the description sets nothing a demonstration could be checked against) 4

Of the 22 not arrived, 11 are past their forecast date, so they are already late, and 11 are not yet due.

Counting the 5 "arguably arrived" milestones as arrived, 17 forecasts can be judged so far: 6 came early and 11 are late, and all 11 late ones are milestones that are overdue and have not arrived. Counting only the one clean arrival, 1 came early and 16 are late. So the totals tip late only because overdue milestones count as late. Of the arrivals alone, all came early.

The split is by kind of task:

  • Early: transcribing speech, reading text aloud, writing simple code, answering open-ended questions from the internet, poker and Atari. Four arrived in about half to four-fifths of the forecast time (ratios 0.56 to 0.80). Two came far earlier: all Atari games (3.8 years against a forecast of 8.8) and poker (0.6 years against 3.6).
  • Late: robots in the physical world (folding laundry, assembling LEGO), beating the best people at particular games (StarCraft, Angry Birds), learning a game from very little play, phone banking, translation, recognising an object from one picture, and two that have demonstrations with more than one gap (the high-school essay and answering factual questions from the internet). Angry Birds was forecast for 2019 and is at least seven years late.

So the experts were too slow on what speech and language models went on to do, and too fast on robots and on narrow game-playing goals that the field stopped chasing.

What the headline rests on

Four rows were "arguably arrived" in the first draft and are now "not arrived", because each names more than one unmet or untested condition and the method allows only one: video from a new viewpoint, the Putnam maths competition, the high-school essay, and answering factual questions from the internet. Two of them (video and Putnam) are not yet due, so moving them changes no early-or-late count. The other two are overdue either way unless their dates count.

The factual-questions row matters most. Its only demonstration is from April 2025, and the agent found nothing earlier that compares a system with people who had internet access on simple factual questions. Its forecast date is 9 September 2023. If John counts it as arrived and an earlier demonstration dated before about September 2023 turns up, it moves from late to early: 7 early and 10 late. If all four rows were counted with their current dates, the totals go back to the first draft's 9 early and 10 late. Either way, the milestones that arrived mostly came early, and the overdue ones are what tip the totals.

Scored by lead time

The ratio is actual time divided by forecast time; under 1 is early.

forecast lead early late, arrived overdue, not arrived not yet due not scored
under 5 years 1 0 1 0 0
5 to 10 years 5 0 9 0 2
10 years or more 0 0 1 11 2

(Generous reading. Four of the six arrivals came within a factor of two of their forecast; the two outside it, Atari and poker, both came far earlier.)

The forecasts of five to ten years ahead are the ones that can be checked now: 5 arrived, all early, and 9 are overdue. Most longer forecasts are not yet due. There are too few yes-or-no events to find a lead beyond which the forecasts stop working, so this ledger records which way the forecasts missed. It does not give a forecast horizon.

Where the agent's judgement matters most

  • Every arrival date means "no later than". The survey asked when a milestone would be feasible for a top lab within a year, and only demonstrations can be seen. This leans every row towards "late".
  • The one-condition rule decides four rows. A row whose demonstration meets the main capability but has two or three gaps fits none of the method's four verdicts exactly: "arguably arrived" allows one gap, and "not arrived" is written for no demonstration of the main capability. The agent put these rows under "not arrived", with the demonstration recorded as the nearest. That is the strict reading of the method, and the method text is unchanged.
  • Which gaps count is itself a judgement. Things the milestone does not state (for example, that a code model learned from public code including sorting routines) are noted in the rows but not counted as gaps.
  • Earlier demonstrations that did not qualify. Microsoft's speech system matched professional transcribers on telephone conversations in October 2016, but leaves both noise and a variety of accents untested, so Whisper (September 2022) still sets the date. Microsoft's March 2018 translation system beat non-professional translators, the milestone's own bar, but for Chinese news only, so translation stays not arrived.
  • Three weak "not arrived" verdicts (translation, phone banking, recognising an object from one picture) rest on not finding a published comparison with people, not on evidence the capability is missing.
  • Atari disagrees with the 2023 scoring for AI Impacts, which said not met. The agent read "all Atari games" as the standard 57-game test set; Agent57 (March 2020) beat the professional games tester's scores on all 57. The 2023 page gives no reason, and that reading may be the difference.

Rulings (2026-10-12)

These were the open questions put to John on 9 October. He ruled on 12 October 2026, in his words "all as recommended": each answer below was the session's recommendation and John approved it, so the authorship is mixed. The questions are kept as they were asked.

  1. The four milestones left unscored (sorting long lists without the form of the solution, grouping unseen objects, answering questions with no definite answer, explaining game moves): give each a bar, or keep them out.

    Ruling: keep them out. They stay out of the scores and their verdict is recorded as "no bar" (it was "left for John" until this ruling). A bar set now would be chosen after seeing the answers, which is the thing the method was written to avoid.

  2. The five "arguably arrived" rows: which count as arrived?

    Ruling: none is promoted. The five rows stay "arguably arrived", and the strict total (only clean arrivals count) and the generous total (arguably arrived counts too) both keep being reported, as the method says.

  3. The one-condition rule: should the method get a fifth verdict (for example "partly arrived") for a demonstration of the main capability with more than one gap, or allow more than one gap under "arguably arrived"? Either is an amendment, and either would bring back the four rows moved on 9 October.

    Ruling: no fifth verdict. The one-condition rule stays as written, and the four rows moved on 9 October (video from a new viewpoint, the Putnam, the high-school essay, answering factual questions from the internet) stay "not arrived" with their nearest demonstration recorded.

  4. The singularity definition's sentence "Milestones from the 2016 Grace survey mostly arrived early" holds for the milestones that arrived, but not for the survey as a whole. Reword it (an amendment), or wait for the rulings above?

    Ruling: amend it now, dated. The sentence now says that the milestones that arrived came early, that most of the survey's milestones have not arrived, and that eleven are overdue. Recorded as amendment A23 in docs/amendments.md, citing this ledger.

  5. Leg B's gauge is defined on forecasts of METR's task-length measure, which this ledger cannot supply. Is this ledger the record of which way forecasts missed and nothing more, or should it feed the gauge somehow?

    Ruling: the record of which way forecasts missed, and nothing more. Leg B's gauge stays defined on the task-length measure, and this ledger does not feed it.

Revisions after the independent check (9 October 2026)

  • Poker re-dated to Libratus, whose match against four top heads-up professionals ended 30 January 2017 (Carnegie Mellon's news release; Science paper). Pluribus (July 2019) is kept in the row as the later, broader result.
  • Speech transcription checked against Microsoft's October 2016 human-parity paper and left on Whisper, because the Microsoft result leaves two conditions untested.
  • Four rows moved from "arguably arrived" to "not arrived" under the one-condition rule.
  • Translation now records Microsoft's March 2018 Chinese-to-English result as its nearest demonstration.
  • Atari now states the 57-game reading.
  • Putnam: the "top human score was 90" figure is the maker's comparison, found only in news coverage; the agent could not open the competition organiser's results page.
