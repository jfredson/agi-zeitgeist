Group e: panel checks under amendment A13 (2020s cohort tier)

Summary: Karnofsky and Cowen both have public AGI statements from before 2018, so both fail the cohort rule. Karnofsky's is a 2016 timing estimate and Cowen's is a 2005 doubt about Kurzweil's Singularity timeline. Cowen looks like a plausible core-panel candidate. Karnofsky is unlikely to qualify for the core panel unless someone finds national-press coverage of him. Leike and Mollick have no public AGI timeline statement before 2018 that I could find, so both pass the cohort rule. Their timing statements are thin, though: they mostly decline to give a date.

Whether a board seat or a staff role counts as "senior" is for John to decide. | skeptic: no. Verdict: fails the cohort rule. He made a dated AGI timing estimate in May 2016, before 2018. Core rule: he meets the early and late statement tests (2016 and 2025), but I found no national-outlet coverage from 2017 or earlier, so his core eligibility is unclear and probably fails on prominence. If he is moved to the core panel, he would count toward the frontier_lab quota. Jan Leike

Tier checked: cohort_2020s C1 earliest: 2019-12-16 | possibility | "I don't think anyone really knows whether that's the case or not." (asked whether deep reinforcement learning will scale to AGI) | https://futureoflife.org/?p=39550 (Future of Life Institute, AI Alignment Podcast transcript) | primary, fetched. The earliest timing statement I found is in the OpenAI superalignment announcement of 2023-07-05, which Leike co-wrote: "While superintelligence seems far off now, we believe it could arrive this decade." How hard I looked: two searches on his work before 2018 (his PhD thesis on general reinforcement learning, Australian National University 2016; his Cambridge talk of 2015-12-16; his 2017 papers on safety test environments and on learning from human preferences). These are technical work on AIXI, a theoretical model of an ideal general agent, and on reward learning. None of it says when AGI arrives. I did not read the full thesis text. C2 latest: 2024-05-17 | "Building smarter-than-human machines is an inherently dangerous endeavor." | https://decrypt.co/231294/openai-exec-quit-safety-took-backseat-shiny-products | secondhand (the article quotes his X thread), fetched. The full trace may find few rows that state a timeline. Quota tags: skeptic: possible, but ambiguous. He is a reasonable core candidate and might count toward the skeptic quota if more statements from before 2017 turn up.
