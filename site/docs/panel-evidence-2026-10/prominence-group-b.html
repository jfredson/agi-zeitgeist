A note on the tools first: the search tool will not filter by nytimes.com, theguardian.com, wired.com, theatlantic.com, newyorker.com, wsj.com or newscientist.com, and the fetch tool cannot open those sites (or vanityfair.com or independent.co.uk). Bylined pieces. Three people below (Russell, Marcus, Brooks) each rely on one piece they wrote or co-wrote themselves in a national outlet. The brief says a person is "named" when "quoted, profiled or cited"; a byline is none of those exactly, so whoever runs the panel rule should decide whether a byline counts. Each person's verdict line says which item it is. If bylines don't count, each of those three has two qualifying pieces, not three.
