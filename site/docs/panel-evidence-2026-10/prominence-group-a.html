Prominence evidence: group-a

Prominence evidence: group-a

Checked against the brief's test: at least three different pieces about where AI is going, in national outlets, naming the person, dated on or before 2017-12-31.

Access notes, which apply to every section:

  • Most major news sites (nytimes.com, theguardian.com, newyorker.com, vanityfair.com, wired.com, economist.com, ft.com, bbc.com, wsj.com, theatlantic.com, newscientist.com) refused both the fetch tool and domain-limited search. Pieces from those outlets are marked seen in search result unless a full copy was readable somewhere else.
  • The New Yorker's "The Doomsday Invention" (2015-11-23) was read in full from a PDF printout of the newyorker.com page, hosted on a USC course reading list. I extracted its text and searched it for every name in this group. It names Kurzweil, Bostrom, Hassabis and Hinton. It does not name Yudkowsky, Hanson, LeCun, Bengio, Legg or Altman. (It refers to the Singularity Institute's founder without giving his name.)
  • Every URL listed came from a fetch or a search result. Where none was found, the entry says so instead of giving a URL.
