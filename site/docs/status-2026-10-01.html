What the seed shows so far (five people only, not a finding)

The "whether, then when, then now" story does not show up in these five. "When will it arrive?" leads in every era: 14 of 21 statements in 2005 to 2015, 14 of 36 in 2016 to 2022, 49 of 100 since 2023. "Is today's system close?" rises from 2016, not 2023 (14 statements in 2016 to 2022). "Can it improve itself?" leads only 5 statements, all since 2023. The clearest change is dates moving closer (Hinton: 2070 in 2015, then 5 to 20 years, then "close to AGI" in 2026), not a change of question.

Options: restrict rejects to "never / not in the frame given" and use present_capability: disputed for "not yet", or add an explicit rule.
