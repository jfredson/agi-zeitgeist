Rule R1b: attribution of posts passed on without comment

When the poster passes on a news item, announcement, demo video, product launch or screenshot of someone else's words, and adds no words of their own about it, attribute the claim to the linked source: the person or organisation whose item is linked or shown (news_outlet for a news article or headline; lab_or_company for a lab's or company's own post, demo or announcement; panel_member or other_person for a person's post or quote). If the post links or shows no identifiable source, attribution = original. This rule covers attribution only; code every other field as the codebook says.
