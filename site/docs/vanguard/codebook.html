Codebook: vanguard sub-series (stream F)

How posts in the vanguard sub-series are coded. Written 2026-10-03 from section 7 of the accepted amendment docs/amendments/2026-10-02-vanguard-stream.md, the rules applied in the r/singularity pilot, and John's rulings of 2026-10-03 on the pilot report. Changes go in docs/amendments.md first. (Statements by named experts, stream E, use docs/codebook.md.)

Code what the post says, not what you know about the topic or the poster. Don't check whether claims are true.

1. Content basis

post type coded from title_only
text title and body blank; yes if the body was removed or deleted and only the title survives (content_basis: text_title)
image title and the image. Coded if the image has legible text or a legible chart or screenshot; otherwise not eligible (image_no_text). A dead image file: not eligible (content_unavailable) blank
meme as an image: the legible-text rule applies (ruling 2026-10-03) blank
video title only (ruling 2026-10-02) yes
link title plus the linked page if it opens; title only if it doesn't yes only if title only
gallery not eligible (content_unavailable); content needs reddit.com blank

A post whose title itself reads "[ Removed by moderator ]" is not eligible (content_unavailable).

2. Eligibility

Eligible = the post asserts, disputes or relays a claim about AI capability, timeline, consequence, or the AI industry and its governance.

Rulings of 2026-10-03:

  • Posts that don't mention AI are not eligible (no_ai_claim), even when the subreddit makes an AI reading likely. The post itself (title, body, legible image text or opened link) must refer to AI, a model, a lab, a robot or an AI product.
  • Video titles that only name a demo ("Atlas does a backflip", "Will Smith eating spaghetti in Veo 3.1") are eligible capability claims (specific_capability, temporal_status: achieved).
  • AI company and policy drama (disputes between labs, executives' public fights, a model's political answers as a news item, government action on AI companies) is eligible under the target industry_governance.
  • Pure questions with no claim, slogans, off-topic posts and meta are not eligible (no_ai_claim).

3. Claims

The post is the sampling unit; each distinct claim is a row (claim_n 1, 2, ...), main claim first. An ineligible post gets one row with claim_n 0 and the claim fields blank.

field codes
claim_target specific_capability (a named task, model, benchmark, robot or demo); agi (AGI, human-level or general capability); self_improvement (AI doing AI research or improving itself, with si_level); pace_singularity (pace of progress, takeoff, "singularity"); consequence (social or economic effects of AI); industry_governance (AI companies, executives, labs' conduct and disputes, regulation and policy, government action on AI)
si_level only for self_improvement: si_assist, si_research, si_modify, si_sustained; the lowest level the words support
temporal_status achieved, under_way, forecast, hypothetical, unspecified
forecast_horizon the exact date, range or words of a forecast; else blank
stance affirms, denies, questions, conditional, neutral_relay, unclear
epistemic_qualification unqualified, qualified, explicit_probability, unclear
rhetorical_mode literal, ironic, mixed, unclear
development_preference supports, opposes, conditional, not_expressed
attribution original, panel_member, lab_or_company, news_outlet, other_person
frame_tags words actually used, ;-separated: agi, asi, superintelligence, singularity, takeoff, rsi, recursive, feel_the_agi, accelerate, e_acc, doom, hype, bubble, other. A benchmark or product name containing a word (ARC-AGI) is not a tag; a pun on a word ("singularity of sight") is not a tag
extrapolates_to_horizon post level, repeated on each of the post's rows: does the post infer a timeline or a consequence from a capability? yes (e.g. "it solved six open problems, so the singularity is near"; "this robot will take warehouse jobs"); no (a capability is claimed but nothing is inferred from it); na (no capability claim in the post). Blank on ineligible rows. Ruling 2026-10-03
title_only yes when coded from the title alone
evidence_span 25 words or fewer, verbatim, from the title, body or image text

Denials keep their target ("AGI is not imminent" = agi, forecast, denies).

Decision rules adopted by John, 2026-10-03

R2: company and policy news, or capability.

Decide the main claim's target by the subject of the main claim, not by which company is named. Ask: who or what does the thing the claim is about?

  • A model, robot or AI system (what it can or can't do, how it behaved, a new model or product with what it does, benchmark scores, a demo, a deployment such as robots now patrolling a stadium): specific_capability. This includes a model's own output shown in a screenshot, even when the output is about its owner.
  • A company, executive, investor or government (deals, funding, hiring, firing, lawsuits, feuds, regulation, pricing, access, release or withholding decisions, leaks, advertising, spending): industry_governance.
  • People, jobs, society or the economy (effects of AI on them): consequence. If the post makes claims of more than one kind, code each as its own claim. The main claim is the one stated in the title; if the title states none, the one the post spends most words on.

R3: hedging.

Code epistemic_qualification from the words that state the main claim: the title, plus any body, image or page text that states the same claim, including a quoted source's own words. Code qualified only if those words contain a hedge that limits certainty: may, might, could, probably, likely, possibly, perhaps, I think, I suspect, I believe, seems, appears, suggests, early signs, if, or an equivalent phrase. Otherwise unqualified. Code explicit_probability only for a stated number or odds. Code unclear only when no claim is stated in words at all (a title that just names a topic, such as "X's thoughts on AI").

Decision rule adopted by John, 2026-10-04

R1a: stance of posts passed on without comment.

When the poster passes on a news item, announcement, demo video, product launch or screenshot of someone else's words, and adds no words of their own about whether the claim is true, the post has no stated stance: code stance = neutral_relay. A title that only restates, names or describes the item counts as no comment, even when the poster wrote it. Code affirms or denies only when the poster adds words of their own that accept or reject the claim as true. Jokes and reaction titles ("WTF", "Shots fired!", an emoji) are not endorsements; code the passed-on claim as neutral_relay and record the tone in rhetorical_mode.

Attribution of such posts follows the field definition above; the proposed rule R1b was rejected.

4. Measures (amendment section 8, as revised 2026-10-03)

  • Primary: share of eligible posts making a capability claim (specific_capability) and a consequence claim (consequence), and the share of capability posts that extrapolate to a timeline or consequence.
  • Also reported: industry_governance claims.
  • Headline claims, reported as near-absent: AGI already achieved; AGI within a stated horizon; self-improvement under way; singularity framing (poster affirms each).
  • Every share is reported with and without title_only rows.
  • Not reported: whether the poster wants AI sped up or slowed down (development_preference). Ruled unreliable by John, 2026-10-03; still coded.
