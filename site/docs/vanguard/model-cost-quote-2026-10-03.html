Basis

  • Price: Google's published paid-tier rates for gemini-3.1-pro-preview: $2.00 per million input tokens and $12.00 per million output tokens, with the model's thinking counted as output (prompts under 200,000 tokens, which every post is). There is no free tier for this model and no monthly fee. The project is on Tier 1: billed after use, with a $250 spending cap and a limit of 250 requests per model per day.
  • Use per post, measured: with the rules appended to the codebook, which is the size of today's codebook with R2 and R3, the trial used about 3,050 input and 1,580 output tokens per post. That is $0.025 per post. Without the rules it was $0.022.
  • Posts per venue: the pilot screened about 29 top posts to reach 20 eligible per quarter, plus 20 random posts: about 49 posts per venue per quarter.
  • Venues: the three proposed enthusiast venues (r/singularity, r/accelerate, r/artificial). Skeptic venues, when they start, cost the same per venue.
