## The note

Panel watch, 2026-10-18. Nick Bostrom, the philosopher whose 2014 book *Superintelligence*
brought the risks of advanced AI to a wide readership, was asked on the AI safety researcher
Roman Yampolskiy's podcast, The Roman Forum, whether OpenAI's GPT-6 is AGI. He called it a
question of definition, then said, "I think it may be less misleading to say that it is an
AGI" (15:17). He added that it still lacks some human abilities, such as learning
continuously, and that superintelligence has not yet been reached.

Earlier this year, in a working paper, Bostrom wrote that full AGI could not yet be launched,
and the tracker recorded that as doubting that today's systems are AGI. This answer leans the
other way, though "full AGI" there may set a higher bar than "an AGI" here.

The interview went out on 2026-10-16. Two days later the tracker's fixed searches for Bostrom
did not turn it up, so these two statements are recorded but kept out of every figure until
the searches find it. agizeitgeist.com
