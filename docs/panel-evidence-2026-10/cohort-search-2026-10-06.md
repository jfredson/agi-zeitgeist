# Search for more 2020s cohort members, 2026-10-06

Asked for by me on 2026-10-06 ("Search first, then freeze"): one to three more cohort
members before the freeze, found at no cost by the same method, with the rules exactly as
written in `docs/methodology-v2.md` section 3:

- **C1:** no public AGI timeline statement found before 2018.
- **C2:** one dated 2024 or later.

Candidates were drawn from the wide list (the MIRI and AI Impacts predictions dataset and
the full TIME100 AI lists read the same day) and from other prominent voices of the 2020s,
preferring people who would broaden the cohort: skeptics, people based outside the United
States and United Kingdom, and academics not at an AI company. Co-written statements were
treated as in A14 ruling 3 (they count when the person is a named author). Quotes were
checked in the raw page text unless marked otherwise. Checked by claude-agent.

Before this search the cohort was 9, all based in the United States or United Kingdom,
and none a skeptic.

### Arvind Narayanan (professor of computer science, Princeton University, United States)

**Verdict: PASS (thin).** Thin because the statement is co-written and gives no arrival
date for AGI.

- **C2: 2025-04-15.** "AI as Normal Technology: An alternative to the vision of AI as a
  potential superintelligence", by Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor, Knight First
  Amendment Institute, 2025-04-15: https://knightcolumbia.org/content/ai-as-normal-technology
  (fetched; quotes checked in the raw page text). **Primary, co-written** (he is a named
  author).

  > "we think that transformative economic and societal impacts will be slow (on the
  > timescale of decades)"

  > "This is related to one definition of artificial general intelligence (AGI)—a unified
  > system that is capable of performing all economically valuable tasks. According to the
  > normal technology view, such sudden economic impacts are implausible. [...] This means
  > that the goalpost of AGI will continually move further away as increasing automation
  > redefines which tasks are economically valuable."

  The essay calls "superintelligent" AI a notion "which we view as incoherent". A
  statement about whether and how fast AGI arrives, doubting it, with a timescale of
  decades for its effects.
- **C1: none found.** The search for 2015 to 2017 statements on AGI, human-level AI or
  superintelligence returned only his work from 2024 on. No pre-2018 AGI statement turned
  up.
- **Tags:** none of the four as defined. Skeptic in outlook (2025), but the tag rests on
  statements from 2017 or earlier. United States. Computer scientist, not a philosopher.
  No AI company role. He broadens the cohort as its first academic skeptic not tied to an
  AI company.
- **Prominence (information only):** TIME100 AI 2023 (with Kapoor); New York Times Hard
  Fork podcast, 2025 (seen in search results).

Queries: `Arvind Narayanan AGI "AI as normal technology" 2025 superintelligence timeline
quote`; `Arvind Narayanan 2015 OR 2016 OR 2017 "artificial general intelligence" OR
"human-level AI" OR superintelligence blog`.
