### Why Floridi and Walsh

1. **The core list already has skeptics working in AI itself:** Gary Marcus, Rodney Brooks, Yann LeCun and Andrew Ng. The gaps are a **philosopher continuing Hubert Dreyfus's critique** and an **AI researcher outside the United States who gives an actual date**. Floridi fills the first and Walsh the second.
2. **Floridi.** His 2016 Aeon essay is the best-known recent restatement of the old philosophical argument: real AI is possible in principle but "utterly implausible". He keeps it up in 2024 with a hype-bubble paper in a leading journal on the philosophy of technology. This gives the "skeptical, and doubts it is even well-defined" category a voice that is not an engineer's.
3. **Walsh.** His 2016 paper argues directly against the idea of a runaway "singularity". His 2018 book puts a year on human-level AI (2062, as the title suggests). He is one of the most-quoted AI scientists in the press in Australia and Britain and is still very active in 2026. A dated forecast plots directly on the "when did each person expect it" chart, which a skeptic who only says "not soon" does not.
4. **Why not the others.**
   - **Davis:** his 2024-and-later statements are almost all co-written with Gary Marcus on Marcus's Substack, so including him would count Marcus twice.
   - **Etzioni:** prominent in 2016, but I could not find a statement from 2024 or later. He is the first backup if Walsh's 2024-and-later record does not hold up.
   - **Mitchell:** prominent, but no record on or before 2017 was found.
5. **Caveat.** Neither proposed skeptic yet has a 2024-or-later statement checked against the full text: for Floridi only the article title was confirmed, and for Walsh nothing was found. Freeze them only after one more search each. If Walsh fails, use Etzioni. If Floridi's article turns out not to discuss AGI, use Davis and note that he overlaps with Marcus.
