## Tim Genewein (added 2026-11-20)

- **Who:** research scientist at Google DeepMind.
- **What:** the essay "What comes after AGI?" on the DeepMind Institute's essay platform, dated
  2026-11-19 (https://institute.deepmind.com/essays/what-comes-after-agi/). It sets out four
  routes from AGI to superintelligence that build on one another: scaling models and data,
  changes of technology, better coordination between AI agents, and AI improving its successors.
  It gives figures for growth in computing (at least ten times a year) and cost (work costing
  $1 billion in computing today at about $1,000 in ten years), but no date and no probability
  for AGI or superintelligence. It says pieces on the platform are not Google's official view.
- **Based on:** the technical report "From AGI to ASI" (arXiv 2606.12683, version 3 of
  2026-11-10; version 1 2026-06-10), 14 authors with Genewein first and Shane Legg last. Legg is
  on the core panel. Whether a co-written report counts as his own statement is an open question
  for John, the same one the weekly reading of 2026-11-16 flagged for Kokotajlo and Narayanan;
  until it is answered, nothing from the report is coded.
- **How it came in:** a tip from John on 2026-11-20, a link to a Verity news story about the essay.
- **Why it may matter for a panel review:** a frontier lab researcher writing at length on how
  superintelligence might follow AGI. On its own it moves no figure.
