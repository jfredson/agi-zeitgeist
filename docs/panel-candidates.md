# Panel candidates

People heard on the questions the tracker asks who are not on any tier of `data/speakers.csv`.
Tip intake step 2 (`docs/tip-intake.md`) and amendment A29 send them here: they are not coded,
their source is archived and listed in `sources/manifest.csv`, and the list is read at the next
panel review. Adding anyone to the panel needs an amendment (A18). Newest first.

## Eli Lifland (added 2026-11-20)

- **Who:** forecaster at the AI Futures Project; a co-author of AI 2027.
- **What:** the lead author of "Q3.5 Timelines Update: Looks Like the Intelligence Explosion
  Will Happen in 2027 After All" (https://blog.aifutures.org/p/q35-timelines-update-looks-like-the,
  2026-11-10), whose byline reads "Eli Lifland, Brendan Halstead, Daniel Kokotajlo, and 3
  others". It moves the group's forecast for an AI that does AI research better than people to
  "likely at some point in 2027". Kokotajlo is on the panel but not the lead, so under A37 the
  post gives no row for him.
- **How it came in:** flagged by the weekly reading of 2026-11-16; settled by A37 on 2026-11-20.

## Tim Genewein (added 2026-11-20)

- **Who:** research scientist at Google DeepMind.
- **What:** the essay "What comes after AGI?" on the DeepMind Institute's essay platform, dated
  2026-11-19 (https://institute.deepmind.com/essays/what-comes-after-agi/). It sets out four
  routes from AGI to superintelligence that build on one another: scaling models and data,
  changes of technology, better coordination between AI agents, and AI improving its successors.
  It gives figures for growth in computing (at least ten times a year) and cost (work costing
  $1 billion in computing today at about $1,000 in ten years), but no date and no probability
  for AGI or superintelligence. It says pieces on the platform are not Google's official view.
- **Based on:** the technical report "From AGI to ASI" (arXiv 2606.12683, version 3 of
  2026-11-10; version 1 2026-06-10), 14 authors with Genewein first and Shane Legg last. Legg is
  on the core panel, but under amendment A37 (2026-11-20) a jointly written piece counts only
  for its lead author, so nothing from the report is coded for him. Genewein, the lead, is on
  no tier.
- **How it came in:** a tip from John on 2026-11-20, a link to a Verity news story about the essay.
- **Why it may matter for a panel review:** a frontier lab researcher writing at length on how
  superintelligence might follow AGI. On its own it moves no figure.
